Personal project

How buyers decide

I read the website of Broker Insights, a data platform for insurance brokers, the way a buyer would, and noted the psychology behind each line that works. I bring the same lens to outreach and pitches.

"850+ broker offices and 26 leading insurers"
Social proofPeers already trust it, so saying yes feels safe.
"Explore, simulate and act on data"
FramingIt sells the decision the buyer gets to make, not the dashboard.
"Gives you back 30% of your time"
Loss aversionStaying with the old way starts to feel like a cost.
"Brokers use only about half their carriers"
Availability biasIt points at a blind spot the buyer hadn't noticed.
